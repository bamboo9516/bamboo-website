EATING

The flesh of haddock is firm and springy. Seasoning with black pepper, chilli power and salt further brings out the fish's sweet, fresh flavour.

Cod is juicy and succulent. Simply Seasoned with salt and lemon juice, it melts in your mouth, bursting with bright, refreshing flavours.

This was my first time trying a meal deal! It was reasonably priced and offered a good balance of nutrients~

My first time ordering Chinese takeaway in the UK. It was so delicious, with amazing colour, aroma and flavour!

The first time inviting friends over to cook at home! The Xinjiang lamb pilaf smelled amazing. Although I couldn't find yellow carrots, it still tasted very authentic!

Got free pizza with my friends near LUU! Such a happy moment!

My first time spotting yellow-sticker items in a supermarket. Grab them, grab them, grab them all!

I had the best salad of my life so far at a local person's home! I really want to ask her how she made it!

The Mid-Autumn Festival, a traditional Chinese festival! Sharing mooncakes with good friends~

One more friend joined our little get-together at my place! So happy!

GIAG's BBQ event! I love taking part in GIAG activities!

I made a big pot of Chinese braised beef using an electric pressure cooker — a major milestone on my journey to becoming a great cook!

After eating too much meat, it's good to have some vegetables now and then to increase my fibre intake and keep my diet balanced!

A nutritious and delicious chicken noodle soup with some fruit on the side, and most importantly, a TV drama that makes the meal even more enjoyable! So happy!

I've been really craving dumplings, but I don't know how to make them, so I had to buy frozen ones instead. Sob sob. They actually tasted pretty good, but I've decided to learn how to make dumplings from scratch when I have some free time!

I finally tried the flatbread at The Refectory that my classmate recommended! So delicious, so delicious, so delicious!

How can the vegetarian dishes taste even better than the meat dishes?! My friends and I were fighting over the vegetables haha!

A mixture of sugar and fat... a delicious combination that humans simply cannot resist...

I can't accept having just one dish with my rice, so even when I'm eating alone, I still have to cook two dishes!!

Beef stir-fried with colourful bell peppers! Can anyone relate?

Flowers from a friend in front of me, food cooked by a friend on the table, and friends by my side. I feel so blessed!

Noodles with rice — carbs go best with more carbs! Another day of being happily knocked out by a carb overload~

So busy! No time to cook... Just making a quick meal out of instant noodles~

Visiting a friend! Our lovely and generous host cooked three dishes for the two of us. We were so full!!!

My friends have crowned me the best Chinese person at making Korean bibimbap!

My first time trying hot chocolate topped with whipped cream! It was unbelievably delicious!!

It was supposed to be the perfect fried egg — runny in the middle, with crispy, golden edges — until I accidentally broke it with my spatula while taking it out of the pan. Noooo! 😭

Oh my goodness! I've found my new favourite dessert!!
